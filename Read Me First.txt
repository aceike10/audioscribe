AudioScribe — transcripts and captions for After Effects
=========================================================

INSTALL ON A MAC
1. Quit After Effects.
2. Double-click "Install AudioScribe.command".
   If macOS says it can't be opened because it's from an unidentified developer:
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway",
   then double-click the file again.
   (Or open Terminal, type "bash " with a space, drag the file into the window,
   and press Return.)
3. Wait for "Done" (about 10 minutes the first time).

INSTALL ON WINDOWS
1. Quit After Effects.
2. Double-click "Install AudioScribe (Windows).bat".
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info > Run anyway.
3. Wait for "Done" (about 10 minutes the first time).

USE IT
Open After Effects > Window > Extensions > AudioScribe.
Select a layer with dialogue and press Transcribe. Click any word to jump there.

Updates install from inside the panel: when one is available, an
"Update to x.y.z" button appears at the top.

Needs After Effects 2022 or later. Running the installer again is safe.
